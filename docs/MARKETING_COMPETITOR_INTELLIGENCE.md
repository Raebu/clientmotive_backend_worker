# Marketing & Competitor Intelligence

ClientMotive treats marketing and competitor intelligence as part of the same commercial decision system as company, market, account, signal and buyer intelligence.

## What the full report covers

The persisted full report generated by `buildMarketingCompetitorIntelligence` covers:

- **Brand strategy** — purpose, promise, category, positioning, differentiation, credibility and brand architecture where evidence exists.
- **Visual identity** — observed colours, theme colour, typography, logo candidates, page design signals and consistency cues. These are observed page signals, not claimed official brand guidelines unless the evidence says so.
- **Verbal identity** — voice, tone, vocabulary, claims, slogans, terminology and message style.
- **Positioning** — category, problem ownership, target audience, differentiation, incumbents, status quo and white-space hypotheses.
- **Audience** — segments, ICPs, likely buyer roles, pains, aspirations, objections, triggers and decision criteria.
- **Customer research** — reviews, complaints, testimonials and public customer evidence where available; private interviews, NPS and churn data are explicitly marked as requiring first-party data.
- **Offer and pricing** — visible products/services, packaging, pricing evidence, trials, guarantees, implementation and time-to-value claims.
- **Messaging architecture** — proposition, benefit hierarchy, proof, objections, buyer/industry/use-case messaging.
- **Website marketing** — proposition clarity, IA, landing pages, CTAs, proof, trust, conversion path and observed personalisation.
- **SEO and content** — topic ownership, content formats, search-visible themes, authority gaps and competitive content opportunities.
- **Social media** — observed channels and public activity signals.
- **Paid advertising** — public evidence only. Spend, ROAS, CAC and conversion are never inferred.
- **Email and lifecycle** — public newsletter/lifecycle evidence where visible; private performance requires first-party data.
- **Outbound and ABM** — commercial hypotheses, target-account logic and evidence-led personalisation principles.
- **Product marketing and GTM** — launches, use cases, routes to market, enablement and category strategy.
- **Campaign and creative strategy** — public campaign/creative themes and gaps where evidence exists.
- **Reputation, PR, events, partnerships and community** — reviews, press, announcements, partnerships, events and authority signals.
- **Customer marketing** — advocacy, proof, expansion/renewal signals where public; private adoption/retention data remains first-party only.
- **Conversion and analytics** — framework and gaps. Private traffic, funnel, attribution and revenue metrics are never guessed.
- **Marketing operations and technology** — public/technical evidence where visible; CRM/CDP/automation configuration requires first-party access.
- **AI governance, legal and compliance** — public policies/claims and gaps.
- **International and employer brand** — localisation, regional signals, hiring/careers and employer-positioning cues.
- **Planning and governance** — public strategy signals plus explicit first-party gaps.

## Full competitor intelligence

ClientMotive distinguishes:

- direct competitors
- indirect / adjacent competitors
- substitutes
- emerging competitors
- consultants / services alternatives
- internal build or manual workarounds
- incumbent systems
- the status quo / doing nothing

For each evidence-supported named competitor, the full report can cover:

- brand colours, fonts and page-design signals
- positioning and category
- target audience / audience overlap
- messaging and claims
- products, services, packaging and visible pricing
- proof points, testimonials and case studies
- website and landing-page strategy
- SEO/content themes
- social presence
- public paid-media evidence
- reputation, reviews and complaints
- launches, partnerships, funding, hiring, M&A and leadership changes
- strengths and weaknesses
- competitive threat
- exploitable gaps
- comparison dimensions
- crowded positions
- white-space opportunities
- evidence URLs and confidence

A named competitor is only included when supplied evidence supports that the organisation exists and is relevant. If evidence is insufficient, ClientMotive stays at category/alternative level rather than inventing a company.

## Data model

Migration `0010_marketing_competitor_intelligence.sql` adds:

- `marketing_intelligence_reports` — the full marketing/competitive report, evidence coverage and confidence.
- `competitor_intelligence_profiles` — per-competitor deep profiles linked to a report.

The standard lead research pipeline now includes the `marketing_intelligence` stage immediately after the existing competitive-landscape stage.

## API

Admin:

- `POST /v1/admin/intelligence/marketing-competitor/analyse`
- `GET /v1/admin/intelligence/marketing-competitor?domain=...`
- `GET /v1/admin/intelligence/marketing-competitor?lead_id=...`

The prospect-safe Growth Snapshot exposes a limited `marketingCompetitive` summary when a full report exists.

The public company preview exposes only a lighter `marketingSnapshot` with observed visual signals, positioning hypothesis, messaging themes, website observations and marketing opportunities. It does not expose private-performance guesses.

## Evidence rules

ClientMotive deliberately does **not** infer private metrics such as:

- CAC
- CPL
- ROAS
- conversion rate
- pipeline attribution
- revenue influenced
- NPS
- churn
- email performance
- private ad spend
- private CRM/marketing automation configuration

Those fields must come from connected first-party data or explicit customer input.

The rule is:

**public evidence → observation → hypothesis → confidence → recommendation → human decision**

not:

**missing data → confident-sounding guess**.
